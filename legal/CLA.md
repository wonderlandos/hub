1.2. It covers two things:
- **Contributions**: code, documentation, translations, artwork, designs and any other material you send to a WolOS repository, by pull request, patch, issue, comment or any other way, with the intent that it becomes part of WolOS.
- **Hub content**: what you send to the hub (listing text, release notes, icons, screenshots, reports, answers).

1.3. It does not cover your Work listed on the hub. Your Work stays under its own licence. The [Publisher Agreement](/terms/publisher) covers listing it.

1.4. If you send a contribution for an organisation, you accept this agreement for yourself and, if an owner of the organisation accepted it on the hub, for the organisation too.

5.1. **Originality.** Each contribution is your own original work, or you have the right to send it under section 3.

5.2. **Other people's work.** If a contribution contains material made by others, you mark it clearly, with its author, its licence and where it came from, and that licence allows it to be used under section 3.

5.3. **Employer and clients.** If an employer, a client, a school or anyone else has rights in what you make, you have their permission to send the contribution under this agreement, or they have given up their rights in it. If this changes, you tell us before your next contribution.

5.5. **Accuracy.** You tell us if you learn that any of these promises is no longer true.

6.1. Moral rights are an author's personal rights, such as the right to be named and to object to changes that harm their honour.

8.1. You must be 16 or older to accept this agreement. You accept it when you sign in to the hub with your GitHub account and press the accept button next to this text, or when a WolOS repository asks you to accept it through the hub before it takes your first contribution.

8.2. When you accept, the hub records your GitHub numeric id, the version of this agreement, the SHA-256 hash of the exact text you were shown, and the time. That record is your signature. It works as an electronic signature under the laws of Brazil, the EU and the US.

8.3. An organisation accepts when one of its owners links it to the hub and accepts this text for it. The record names the owner who accepted.

8.4. A new version applies only after you accept it. Until then, your earlier contributions stay under the version you accepted. The hub asks you to accept the new version before your next contribution or action that needs it.
