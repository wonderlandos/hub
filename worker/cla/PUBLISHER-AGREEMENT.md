# The WolOS Hub Publisher Agreement

Version 1, 2026-11-04.

You accept this page before you publish anything on the WolOS Hub for the first time. It does not take your work from you: you keep everything you made, and you keep your licence.

## 1. Words used here

"You" is the GitHub account that accepts this page. When you publish for an organisation, you also act for it. "We" is the WolOS project, decided by its authority: today Julia Klee as lead, in the future the councils or maintainers the project formally appoints. "Your work" is every app, plugin and extension you list, and every version of it.

## 2. What you promise

- You have the right to publish your work under the licence you name. It is yours, or its authors allow it.
- The licence you name is the one that applies. Anyone may use your work under it.
- Your work does what its listing says, and nothing it hides. No malware, no tracking you did not tell people about, no taking files or accounts that are not yours.
- You are responsible for your code, every version of it, and what it does on people's machines.
- You keep your signing keys and your GitHub account where only you can reach them. If either may be in someone else's hands, you tell us at once.

## 3. What you allow us

- To list your work: show its name, summary, licence, version, owner and source repository, and link to the release file on GitHub. We do not host your files; people download them from your GitHub release.
- To check every version: download it, compute its hash, open it and run the hub's automatic checks on it. The result, with what was found, is public.
- To sign the index that lists your work, and to keep a public log of every index, check result, certificate and withdrawal.
- To flag a version, refuse it, or withdraw a listing or any version of it, at any time and without notice, when we think it breaks this agreement, the Doctrine or the law, when its repository changes hands, or when we suspect your keys or account are no longer only yours.

None of this is a licence to your work beyond what your own licence already gives everyone.

## 4. What a listing says, and what it does not

A listing says what was checked: that the file came from the repository shown, that its owner is the GitHub account or organisation shown, and what the automatic checks found in that version. It does not say the software is free of faults or fit for any purpose. We do not test, review or vouch for your work, and we never call it anything more than checked.

## 5. No warranty, limited liability

The hub is offered as it is, with no warranty of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for any loss that comes from your work, from a listing, or from its withdrawal.

## 6. Ending it

You can stop at any time: withdraw your listings, or ask us to. What was already signed and logged stays in the public log, because that log is how people check what they installed. We can stop listing your work if you break this agreement. Serious cases, such as malware or threats, end at once.

## 7. Changes, disputes and contact

We change this page only by a new version; you see the new text before you publish again. Takedowns, disputes and lost keys: julia@wolos.org, as fast as you can. Conduct: conduct@wolos.org.

This page is read and applied by the project's authority. Each version is a commit in the hub's repository, and the hub shows the hash of the exact text you accept.
