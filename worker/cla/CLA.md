# The WolOS Hub contributor agreement

Version 1, 2026-11-04.

You accept this page when you sign in to the WolOS Hub (hub.wolos.org) for the first time. It is short on purpose. Publishing has its own page, the Publisher Agreement, and certificates have theirs, the Trust Terms.

## 1. Who agrees to what

"You" is the GitHub account that accepts this page, or the GitHub organisation an owner accepts it for. "We" is the WolOS project, decided by its authority: today Julia Klee as lead, in the future the councils or maintainers the project formally appoints.

## 2. How you take part

- You follow the Wonderland Doctrine (wolos.org/doctrine) everywhere on the hub: listings, release notes, reports and answers to people.
- You report in good faith. A report says what you saw, where and when. Reports are not a weapon against someone you disagree with.
- You use the hub as yourself. One person, their own GitHub account. No accounts made to get around a decision.

## 3. What you send us

What you write on the hub (a report, a listing's name and summary, an answer) may be shown, stored and moderated by us. You keep it; you allow us to show it on the hub and in its public log. A report's text is seen only by you and the moderators, and we never publish who reported.

## 4. What we keep about you

Your GitHub id, login, name and avatar; the organisations GitHub says you belong to; what you accepted and when; what you listed and reported. We store no IP address and no password. Your GitHub sign-in stays sealed inside your own browser cookie.

## 5. What we may do

We may warn you, remove what you wrote, or stop your account from using the hub, as the Doctrine's enforcement section describes. Most problems end with a private word.

## 6. Changes and contact

We change this page only by a new version, and you see the new text before you go on. Questions and disputes: julia@wolos.org. Conduct: conduct@wolos.org.

This page is read and applied by the project's authority, like the Doctrine. Each version is a commit in the hub's repository, and the hub shows the hash of the exact text you accept.
